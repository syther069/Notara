Questions

Does Notara hold my money?
No. Money goes from the customer's wallet to your account. Payments from other chains are routed by NEAR Intents, which delivers USDC to you on NEAR.
What does the wallet signature prove?
That the link's amount, recipient and description were approved by the wallet that gets paid. It shows who is asking, not that they will deliver.
What can I try for free?
Payment links, wallet payments, send links, scam reports and the AI-agent vault all run on NEAR testnet with free test money. Paying from another chain needs mainnet.
Is it audited?
No. Notara is a hackathon prototype, so keep mainnet amounts small.
